## High — a reader following along hits this

**H1. Four chapters check out their own finished tag, then ask the reader to build
what it already contains.**
Chapter 16 and chapter 25 handle this ("check out `ch15` to type along" / "one tag
behind, on purpose"). These four don't:

- **ch19** (`ch19:52`) checks out `ch19`, which already has the `alertmanager` and
  `pager` jobs, `meta-alerts.yml` (5 rules) and the `deadman` route.
  - "Add both to `prometheus.yml`" (`:357`) duplicates a `job_name`, so `make check`
    fails.
  - The gray "No heartbeat yet" header (`:66–72`) can't appear, because the Watchdog
    is already wired up.
- **ch20** (`ch20:45`) checks out `ch20`, where the `snip-slo` group, `docs/SLO.md`
  and the policy already exist. The chapter says to create all three.
- **ch21** (`ch21:48`) checks out `ch21`. The guards are already in
  `snip-burnrate.yml`, and the near-idle test case is already in the test file. So
  "expect it to fail" (`:752`) passes instead, and `SnipHighErrorRatio` is already
  deleted.
- **ch23** (`ch23:46`) checks out `ch23`, where the `blackbox-exporter` and
  `redis-exporter` services and the ip4-fixed `blackbox/config.yml` already exist.
  - "Add the service to `lab/compose.yaml`" (`:201`) creates a duplicate YAML key.
  - The `preferred_ip_protocol: ip6` trap (`:253`) can't reproduce.

Fix: start each one on the previous tag, or say "read along on `chNN`, or check out
`chNN-1` to type it", the way ch16 does.

This is `STATUS.md` item V2 ("owes chapter 22 its worked example"), plus the

**H3. Three contradictory stories about when two Prometheus flags arrive.** The tags
show `--web.enable-otlp-receiver` arriving in ch28 and `--enable-feature=exemplar-storage`
in ch29. But:

- `ch25:487–501` prints both flags in the `command:` block the reader is told to type
  in ch25, and says they "belong to Chapters 28 and 29".
- `ch25:712–720`: the security table says the OTLP receiver is on "because earlier
  chapters needed them".
- `ch29:263`: "`lab/compose.yaml` has carried it since Chapter 1".
- `app-i-solutions.md:1040`: says both flags are present at ch11.

Fix: drop both flags from ch25's block, and correct ch29:263 and solution 11.6.

**H4. Appendix C's `@ end()` idiom is a parse error** (`app-c-promql-by-type.md:156`):

```text
topk(5, sum by (route) (rate(...[$__rate_interval])) @ end())
```

promtool 3.13.1 reports: `@ modifier must be preceded by an instant vector selector or
range vector selector or a subquery`. The `@` has to go on the selector, inside the
`rate()`.

**H5. The ch21 low-traffic alert is inverted** (`ch21:915–918`). "Alert on the
valid-event rate itself" gives `job:snip_sli_valid:rate5m >= 60 / 3600`, which fires
whenever traffic is *healthy*. It should be `< 60 / 3600`. (The same expression in
Appendix I, ex. 21.5, is used as a guard clause, and there it's correct.)
